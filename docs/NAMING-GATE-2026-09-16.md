# Naming Gate — 2026-09-16

## Gate verdict

`NO_RENAME_YET`

SignalForge remains the canonical product/runtime identifier for now.

The reason is not attachment to the existing name. The six initial replacement directions all fail the collision/confusion bar after a current web + GitHub screen. Renaming now would trade a slightly imperfect semantic fit for a materially worse naming-risk profile.

No repository, Worker, API, X-Agent slug, package, route, or documentation-wide rename is authorized by this gate.

## Product naming thesis

The product has evolved beyond a conventional trading-signal generator. Its differentiating mechanism determines whether available evidence is sufficient and admissible for an agent to form a conclusion, while preserving refusal, recovery, freshness, lineage, and receipt boundaries.

Canonical memory sentence:

> Prove the right to conclude.

A replacement name should therefore evoke at least one of:

- evidence admissibility
- justified / grounded conclusions
- a visible decision boundary
- refusal until proof conditions are met
- inspectable lineage / receipts
- bounded validity rather than permanent truth

It should not imply autonomous execution authority.

## Initial candidates screened

### CLEARANCE — FAIL

Why it fit:

- directly expressed granting/refusing permission
- strong relationship to the visible gate

Why it fails:

- `Clearance AI` already exists for regulated medical-device compliance workflows
- another `ClearanceAI` exists for AI deployment evaluation in regulated industries
- a recent Devpost product named `Clearance` is explicitly an AI-agent approval/spend-control gate
- GitHub already contains established repositories using the exact `clearance` name

Collision severity: `CRITICAL`

### THRESHOLD — FAIL

Why it fit:

- maps naturally to evidence sufficiency and gate crossing

Why it fails:

- multiple active software products use Threshold
- one current product is specifically an MCP gateway that evaluates every AI-agent tool call and returns EXECUTE / PAUSE / BLOCK
- other active Threshold products cover AI usage governance, trust infrastructure, business agents, home software, underwriting, and creator scoring

Collision severity: `CRITICAL`

### PROOFLATCH — FAIL

Why it fit:

- strong mechanical metaphor: remain locked until proof is sufficient
- highly compatible with the TRACE visual direction

Why it fails:

- an active project already uses the exact name `ProofLatch`
- its product story is extremely adjacent: deterministic evidence gates, BLOCKED/READY states, receipts, model explanation, and new evidence reopening the latch
- exact GitHub repository exists: `pakales/prooflatch`

Collision severity: `CRITICAL`

### RIGHT TO ACT — FAIL

Why it fit:

- memorable expression of authority boundaries

Why it fails:

- a 2026 paper already defines `Right-to-Act` as a pre-execution non-compensatory decision protocol for AI systems
- the conceptual overlap is direct enough that use as our brand could imply derivation, equivalence, or ownership of that external protocol
- it also over-weights action/execution, while SignalForge currently proves a narrower `right to conclude` and keeps execution authority external

Collision severity: `CRITICAL`

### GROUNDCLEAR — FAIL

Why it fit:

- grounding + clearance is semantically strong for agents

Why it fails:

- `GroundClear` is an established Ortho product family with substantial retail visibility
- even though the sector is unrelated, the exact lexical collision is strong and would make search/discovery unnecessarily noisy

Collision severity: `HIGH`

### ADMITLINE — FAIL

Why it fit:

- closely maps to the evidence admission ledger
- GitHub exact-name repository search returned no repository at screening time

Why it fails:

- `AdmitLine` is already an active named software module in MedAppz healthcare software, used for advised hospital admissions through bed placement
- the word also naturally drifts toward school/hospital admissions rather than epistemic evidence admission

Collision severity: `HIGH`

## Adjacent naming territory screened

### CLAIMBOUND — DO NOT USE

An active AI/evidence project and commercial service already use ClaimBound/Claimbound for evidence-backed claims and reproducible claim checks. This is extremely close to our evidence-bound conclusion space.

### WARRANT / WARRANTGATE territory — DO NOT USE AS-IS

`Warrant` is already an active AI-agent evidence infrastructure company focused on regulator-grade per-action records. `WarrantGate` also appears as a named component in a 2026 evidential-admission/RAG preprint. The underlying epistemic term is excellent, but this lexical territory is already crowded in exactly the adjacent market.

## Naming requirements for the next generation

A new candidate must pass all of these before migration discussion:

1. **Semantic fit** — closer to evidence-grounded conclusion authority than `SignalForge`.
2. **Low adjacent collision** — no same/near-same AI governance, evidence, agent-control, trading, or verification product.
3. **Search distinctiveness** — should be discoverable without competing with a dominant unrelated consumer brand.
4. **Pronounceability** — understandable when spoken once in a demo.
5. **Memory** — judges should be able to recall it after a short demo.
6. **Visual fit** — must support the Prismatic Evidence Foundry identity without forcing generic shield/checkmark branding.
7. **Mechanism fit** — should work with admission, lineage, lease, refusal, recovery, verification, and receipt language.
8. **Authority truth** — must not imply that the product itself executes, certifies truth, guarantees profitability, or grants legal/regulatory approval.
9. **Migration viability** — repository, Worker, API/X-Agent slug, docs, screenshots, receipts, and submission links must be migratable without breaking proof continuity.
10. **Preliminary trademark/domain screen** — must pass before any public rename. This gate is product research, not legal clearance.

## Current decision

`SignalForge` remains canonical.

This is a hold, not a permanent endorsement of the name.

The next naming pass should generate a fresh family rather than recycle obvious `proof`, `evidence`, `threshold`, `clearance`, `warrant`, or `right-to-act` compounds. Those semantic neighborhoods are already unusually crowded in 2026.
