# SignalForge Demo Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Technical, evidence-first product film with a light laboratory canvas, prismatic signal accents, crisp editorial typography, and restrained motion. The visual language should feel inspectable and engineered rather than cinematic-for-cinema's-sake: proof cards, provenance rails, structured grids, and deliberate emphasis on refusal states.

## Colors

- `#EEF3FF` — primary canvas
- `#171522` — ink / authority boundary
- `#3257FF` — primary evidence accent
- `#00C9E8` — secondary signal accent
- `#C6F432` — verified / admitted state
- `#FF4F73` — refusal / contradiction state
- `#B9C6F2` — borders and grid structure

## Typography

- Primary: Inter, Arial, sans-serif
- Evidence / runtime values: monospace

## Motion

- 30 fps master.
- Fast evidence reveals, not trailer-style zooms.
- Use ease-out entrances and short crossfades.
- Refusal states should land decisively and then hold long enough to read.
- Product screenshots remain the dominant proof surface after the opener.

## What NOT to Do

- No dark-blue/black generic AI SaaS aesthetic.
- No fake terminal noise or synthetic trading charts presented as evidence.
- No gratuitous particles, random motion, or infinite loops.
- No generated screenshots masquerading as live product state.
- No profitability, execution-authority, or forecast-certainty claims.
